## Why they need a website
1. **Money:** 3 campuses, Playgroup to FSc, fee from hundreds of students.
2. **Customers:** parents compare schools online before admissions; competitors show up, Beacon doesn't.
3. **High demand moment:** spring admissions (Feb–Apr) are coming. A site with campuses, classes, fee enquiry and online admission form is needed before then.
4. Facebook page has only ~1.6K likes: weak online presence for a 3-campus school.

## Site plan (concept)
- Style: Minimalism + Bento Grid (trustworthy, organised), school colours from logo.
- Sections: hero "Admissions Open 2027" + apply/WhatsApp buttons → 3 campuses with maps → classes (Playgroup–FSc, pre-cadet) → why Beacon (English, digital learning, activities) → results and achievements → gallery → online admission form → contact.
