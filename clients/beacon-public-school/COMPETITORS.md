## Lessons
1. **PPSC is the bar to beat.** Fast, clear, full of proof, with portal, FAQ, scholarships and campus pages. Beacon's site must at least match this, or the pitch "better than competitors" fails.
2. **PEACE** shows what parents respond to locally: board toppers, MDCAT results and scholarships, but buried inside a heavy popup poster image.
3. **Superior** proves a generic chain page loses to a focused local site.

## Gaps NO competitor fills (Beacon's advantage)
1. **Bilingual English + Urdu** toggle. Many Haripur parents read Urdu more comfortably; no competitor offers this.
2. **Fee transparency**: a "fee range per class" table or instant fee estimate on WhatsApp. Nobody shows fees.
3. **Transport / van routes** per campus.
4. **One-tap WhatsApp admission form**: parent fills class + campus + name, message lands on Beacon's WhatsApp pre-filled.
5. **Campus finder**: 3 campuses (Shakar Shah Road, TIP Housing Colony, Mankrai) with map, timings and classes offered at each.
6. **Results wall** built as real text (not poster images) so Google can read it.
7. Speed target: lighter than PPSC (under ~150 KB first load), all images with alt text, one H1, meta description.

## Must-have list for Beacon (match PPSC + beat it)
- Admissions Open banner + Apply / WhatsApp / Call buttons
- Stats strip (years, students, campuses, results), real numbers from Beacon
- Programmes: Playgroup to FSc, pre-cadet classes
- 3 campuses with maps
- Results & achievers, scholarships
- Gallery, principal message, FAQ
- Urdu toggle, fee range table, transport routes (the 3 extras)
