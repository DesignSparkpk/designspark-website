## Why it feels professional
1. **One page per parent question.** Each page has its own Google title, so it ranks for "admissions", "teaching jobs", "results" separately.
2. **Answer-first intro** on every page: one paragraph that answers the question (good for Google and AI search), then an "On this page" menu.
3. **Trust content:** founder message with name, leadership team, founding year, student count, real board results with pass rate and A+ count.
4. **Safety for parents:** CCTV, ID-card pick-up, SMS absence alerts, written rules.
5. **Clear admissions:** steps, documents, age table, scholarships, long FAQ.
6. **Same CTA band at the bottom of every page** ("Admissions open, Apply online, WhatsApp") and a rich footer.
7. **Digital extras:** LMS parent portal, WhatsApp channel, careers portal, alumni network.

## Where Beacon already beats it
- English + Urdu toggle (PPSC is English only, even though its office "helps in Urdu")
- Fee section and transport routes (PPSC has neither)
- WhatsApp admission form that sends a ready message (PPSC uses a separate portal)
- Faster and lighter page, Lighthouse 99-100
