Privacy policy

This website does not store your personal information. The forms only prepare your message in WhatsApp, and you decide whether to send it.

What information we collect

The name, phone number and message you type in the admission and contact forms are not saved on any server. When you press the button, they open as a message in your own WhatsApp.

How we use it

Information received on WhatsApp is used only to handle admissions and answer your question. We do not sell or share it with third parties.

Language preference

If you choose Urdu, that choice is saved only in your browser so the site opens in Urdu next time.

Other websites

This website links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Google Maps and BISE Abbottabad, which have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact 0300-5148824.
