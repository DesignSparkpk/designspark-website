Admissions

Admissions are open in all classes at Beacon Public School & College, from Playgroup to FSc, plus pre-cadet classes for Grades 5 to 9. Get the admission form from any campus, call 0300-5148824, or send the online form below. Seats are limited, so apply early.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Are admissions open now?

Yes. Admissions are open in all classes from Playgroup to FSc. Seats are limited, so get in touch early.

Which classes does Beacon offer?

From Playgroup, Nursery and Prep through Grades 1 to 10 (Matric) and FSc, plus pre-cadet classes for Grades 5 to 9.

What are pre-cadet classes?

A new programme for Grades 5 to 9, launched in 2026, built on discipline, academics and character. Message us on WhatsApp for details.

Where are Beacon's campuses?

Main Campus on Shakar Shah Road, TIP Campus on Housing Colony Road No 18, and Mankrai Campus near BHU. The Montessori Campus is on Main Shakar Shah Road.

Which board is Beacon affiliated with?

Beacon is registered with BISE Abbottabad (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Abbottabad).

Are junior and senior sections separate?

Yes. Junior and senior sections have separate campuses. Contact us to find the right campus for your class.

How much is the fee?

The fee depends on the class. Message 0300-5148824 on WhatsApp for your child's exact fee.

Is school transport available?

Ask about your area's route on WhatsApp.

Where do I get the admission form?

At any campus, or send the online form on this page and the admission office will contact you.
