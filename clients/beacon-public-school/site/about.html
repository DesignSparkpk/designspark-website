About Beacon

Beacon Public School & College is a private school and college in Haripur, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, teaching from Playgroup to FSc. It has three campuses, on Shakar Shah Road, in TIP Housing Colony and in Mankrai, and is registered with BISE Abbottabad.

Recognition

A household name in Haripur

A local guide to schools in Haripur lists Beacon Public School among the city's top schools and calls it a household name in Haripur, for its focus on English, digital learning and activities such as debates and science fairs.

Beacon describes itself as an institution that achieves board positions every year.
