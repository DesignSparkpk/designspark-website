About Beacon

Beacon Public School & College is a private school and college in Haripur, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, teaching from Playgroup to FSc on three campuses. It is registered with BISE Abbottabad.

Our motto Values At a glance Recognition
Our motto

Aspire · Acquire · Inspire

These three words sit on the Beacon crest and describe every student's journey.

Aspire

Dream big and set high goals.

Acquire

Gain knowledge and skills through hard work and discipline.

Inspire

Become an example for family, community and country.

Mission & values

We don't just teach

We inspire, guide and empower.

  • Academics and personal growthAcademic excellence with personal development, preparing students for higher education and life, not only exams.
  • Discipline and creativityA focus on discipline, creativity and holistic growth.
  • Character buildingThrough sports, art, leadership and co-curricular activities.
  • A safe environmentA safe and supportive place to learn, with separate junior and senior campuses.
