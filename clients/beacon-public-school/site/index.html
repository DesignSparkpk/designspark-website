Welcome to Beacon

A household name in Haripur

Beacon Public School & College teaches from Playgroup to FSc on its campuses at Shakar Shah Road, TIP Housing Colony and Mankrai in Haripur, and is registered with BISE Abbottabad.

We don't just teach. We inspire, guide and empower.

  • Board positionsAn institution that achieves positions in the BISE Abbottabad board every year.
  • Experienced facultyDedicated, experienced teachers focused on every student's success.
  • Smart classroomsModern learning tools, libraries and digital learning.
  • Discipline and characterAcademic excellence with co-curricular activities and character building.
  • Separate, safe campusesSeparate campuses for junior and senior sections, in a safe and supportive environment.
