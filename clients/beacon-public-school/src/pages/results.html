Guide for parents

How to check a BISE Abbottabad result

  1. Open the board websitebiseatd.edu.pk
  2. Choose the examSSC (Matric) or HSSC (FSc) and the year
  3. Enter the roll numberThe number printed on the roll number slip
  4. See the resultFor help, message the school on WhatsApp
