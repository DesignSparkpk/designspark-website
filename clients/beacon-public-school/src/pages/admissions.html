FAQ

Admission questions

Are admissions open now?

Yes, in all classes from Playgroup to FSc. Seats are limited, so get in touch early.

Which classes does Beacon offer?

From Playgroup, Nursery and Prep through Grade 10 (Matric) and FSc, plus pre-cadet classes for Grades 5 to 9.

What are pre-cadet classes?

Special classes for students in Grades 5 to 9, built on discipline, academics and character.

Where are the campuses?

Main and Montessori campuses on Shakar Shah Road, TIP Campus on Housing Colony Road No 18, and Mankrai Campus near BHU.

Which board is Beacon affiliated with?

BISE Abbottabad (Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Abbottabad).

Are junior and senior sections separate?

Yes. Contact us to find the right campus for your child's class.

How do I find out fees and transport?

Message 0300-5148824 on WhatsApp with your child's class and your area.
