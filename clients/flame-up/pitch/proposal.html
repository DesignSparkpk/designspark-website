How a customer orders

  1. Watches a Flame Up video on TikTok and taps the link in bio
  2. Picks deals and pizzas on the website
  3. Chooses delivery, pick-up or dine-in and adds the address
  4. Presses Send: the order and total arrive on Flame Up's WhatsApp
